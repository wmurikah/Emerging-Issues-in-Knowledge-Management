# Contributing

Contributions that improve the accuracy, clarity, discoverability, or research value of this repository are welcome.

Useful contributions include:

- corrections to factual or technical statements;
- relevant academic or practitioner references;
- clearer explanations of knowledge-management concepts;
- examples of emerging knowledge-management practices;
- accessibility improvements;
- metadata, structured-data, or search-discoverability improvements;
- corrections to broken links or outdated references.

## Contribution principles

1. Distinguish established facts from interpretation or opinion.
2. Prefer primary, academic, standards-based, or otherwise authoritative sources where practical.
3. Do not add invented citations, DOIs, statistics, or empirical findings.
4. Keep the writing clear enough for both academic and practitioner audiences.
5. Preserve the repository's focus on knowledge management and closely related information-systems issues.
6. Avoid keyword stuffing or other search-engine manipulation. Discoverability should come from useful, well-structured content.

## Pull requests

Please describe what you changed and why. For substantive factual additions, include the source used to support the change.
