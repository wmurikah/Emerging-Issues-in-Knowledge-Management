Research direction

Contemporary extensions

Current knowledge-management research increasingly intersects with artificial intelligence, generative AI, semantic technologies, knowledge graphs, digital workplaces, data governance, cybersecurity, privacy and responsible AI. These areas should be assessed against established knowledge-management principles rather than treated as isolated technology trends.

This repository is designed as a living research resource. Additional peer-reviewed references can be added as the project develops, with preference for primary research and authoritative institutional sources.
