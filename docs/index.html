Emerging Issues in
Knowledge Management

Artificial intelligence, knowledge graphs, digital transformation and organizational learning are changing how organizations create, govern, retain and reuse knowledge.

Explore the research Open K-FRAME View repository

Wilberforce Murikah · First published March 2025 · Updated September 2026

Context

Knowledge is becoming conversational, connected and machine-assisted.

Traditional knowledge-management systems were designed primarily to capture, store and retrieve documents. Emerging systems increasingly combine semantic relationships, machine learning, large language models, collaboration platforms and automation. These capabilities can make organizational knowledge easier to discover and reuse, while creating new questions about provenance, quality, access, privacy, security, accountability and human judgement.

This research resource organizes those issues for students, researchers, information-systems professionals, knowledge-management practitioners and technology leaders.

Research themes

01

Artificial intelligence

AI can augment knowledge discovery, summarization, classification and decision support, but generated knowledge must be governed for accuracy and provenance.

02

Knowledge graphs

Semantic relationships can connect people, concepts, documents and systems, enabling richer discovery than isolated repositories.

03

Organizational learning

Knowledge becomes valuable when teams absorb, share and embed it into routines, systems and decisions.

04

Knowledge retention

Organizations need deliberate ways to preserve institutional memory when experienced people change roles or leave.

05

Digital transformation

Cloud platforms, automation and integrated workflows are reshaping how knowledge moves across organizational boundaries.

06

Governance and trust

Quality, ownership, access, security, privacy, explainability and accountability increasingly determine whether knowledge systems can be trusted.
