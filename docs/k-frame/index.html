      Method

      From maturity assessment to knowledge stress testing

      K-FRAME treats organizational knowledge as a dependency system. The production model is designed to map critical knowledge to people, evidence, systems, processes and decisions, then simulate failure across those relationships.

      F

      Fragility

      Measures concentration, undocumented know-how and single points of knowledge failure.

      R

      Resilience

      Measures redundancy, transfer readiness, recovery and continuity under disruption.

      A

      Assurance

      Measures whether knowledge claims and decisions can be verified against current authority.

      M

      Memory

      Measures whether rationale, exceptions and lessons remain reconstructable over time.

      E

      Evidence

      Measures provenance, freshness, consistency and suitability for human and AI use.

      Production path

      Designed to become a private web product.

      The public prototype uses a deliberately simplified browser heuristic. The proposed commercial implementation keeps scoring weights, graph inference, calibration data, recommendation ranking and private prompts behind an authenticated API. That architecture can later run on a dedicated Murikah subdomain without exposing the intelligence layer in this public repository.
