      Method

      From knowledge-management maturity assessment to knowledge stress testing

      K-FRAME treats organizational knowledge as a dependency system. It asks whether knowledge remains available, trustworthy and reproducible under disruption, rather than asking only whether documentation, technology or knowledge-sharing practices exist.

      F

      Fragility

      Measures concentration, undocumented know-how and single points of knowledge failure.

      R

      Resilience

      Measures redundancy, knowledge transfer, recovery and continuity under disruption.

      A

      Assurance

      Measures whether knowledge claims and decisions can be verified against current authority.

      M

      Memory

      Measures whether rationale, exceptions and lessons remain reconstructable over time.

      E

      Evidence

      Measures provenance, freshness, consistency and suitability for human and AI use.

      Research project

      Use K-FRAME, intervene, then test it again.

      The study design uses a baseline assessment, a selected knowledge shock, targeted remediation and a repeated test. The research then compares model improvement with observable outcomes such as recovery time, handover success, decision reproduction and source provenance.
